What does health insurance cover?

It covers medical and hospital expenses, doctor visits, prescriptions and tests, depending on the plan you choose. Some plans also include preventive care. It gives you financial support when you need to take care of your health.

What is supplemental insurance?

It's additional coverage that complements your main insurance and gives you financial support for medical expenses or specific situations that aren't fully covered. It can reduce your out-of-pocket costs and give you more protection.

What is life insurance and why should I have it?

Life insurance is a financial protection tool that lets your loved ones receive financial support if you pass away. It's a way to protect your family, help keep them financially stable and give them peace of mind for the future.

What types of life insurance are there?

Term life

Provides coverage for a specific period, usually 10, 20 or 30 years. If the insured person passes away during that term, the beneficiaries receive the amount set in the policy. It's usually more affordable than other types of life insurance.

Final expense

Designed to help cover funeral costs, cremation, small debts or final medical expenses. These are lower-amount policies, generally affordable and with permanent coverage.

IUL (Indexed Universal Life)

Permanent life insurance that combines protection with a savings component whose growth is linked to certain market indexes. It lets you build cash value that you may be able to use during your lifetime, according to the policy terms.
